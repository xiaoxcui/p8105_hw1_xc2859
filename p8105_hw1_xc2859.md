**Describe the dataset**

The `penguins` dataset contains 8 important variables: species, island,
bill_length_mm, bill_depth_mm, flipper_length_mm, body_mass_g, sex,
year.

The categorical variables include: `species` (Adelie, Chinstrap, and
Gentoo), `island` (Biscoe, Dream, and Torgersen), and `sex` (female and
male).  
The numeric variables include: `bill_length_mm` (range from 32.1, 59.6
mm), `bill_depth_mm` (range from 13.1, 21.5 mm), `flipper_length_mm`
(range from 172, 231 mm), `body_mass_g` (range from 2700, 6300 g), and
`year` (study year of 2007, 2008 and 2009).

The dataset contains 344 rows and 8 columns.

The mean flipper length is 200.9152047 mm.
